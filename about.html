Who we are

DataQuant is a South African data consultancy. We work with organisations that know their data could do more for them, but do not have the time or the team to make it happen.

We combine data engineering, analytics and security in one team. That means the systems we build are designed to be useful and secure from the start, not fixed afterwards.
