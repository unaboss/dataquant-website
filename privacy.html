Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information.

DataQuant respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you contact us, we collect the details you give us: your name, organisation, email address, phone number and message.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver our services. We do not sell your information or send you marketing without your consent.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and then delete them unless you become a client.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).
