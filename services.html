Services

Three services. Every engagement fits one of them.

Each service answers one part of our promise: use your data, automate your work, and keep that data safe.

01 · Use your data

Data Strategy & Analytics

For organisations that have data but are not getting answers from it. We work out what matters, then make it visible.

  • Data assessmentWhat data you hold, where it lives and how reliable it is.
  • Data strategy and roadmapA prioritised plan for what to build first and why.
  • Reporting and dashboardsLive dashboards and automated reports for decision-makers.
  • AnalysisAnswers to specific business questions from your own data.
Example: a business with sales spread across spreadsheets gets one dashboard that updates itself every morning.
02 · Automate your work

Data Engineering

For organisations where people spend their day moving information by hand. We build the systems that do it for them.

  • Systems and applicationsCustom systems built around your data, such as booking, ordering or job tracking.
  • IntegrationsConnecting the tools you already use so data flows between them.
  • Data pipelines and databasesCollecting, cleaning and storing data in one trusted place.
  • Intelligent automationUsing AI where it helps, for example reading documents or answering enquiries.
Example: a guesthouse gets a booking system that captures enquiries, keeps guest records in one place and syncs with its calendar.
03 · Keep that data safe

Data Security & Governance

For organisations that hold personal or sensitive information and need to know it is protected.

  • POPIA complianceMapping personal information, policies, consent and Information Officer duties.
  • Security testingAuthorised testing of websites and systems, with a plain-language report.
  • Data governanceWho owns which data, who can access it and how long it is kept.
  • Security for what we buildEvery system we deliver is security-tested before it goes live.
Example: a practice holding client records gets a POPIA review, a fix list and a tested website.
